# Kerby Bennett

PhD Candidate in Anthropology, UCLA · Research Program Manager, USC

I work where ethnographic inquiry meets the operational side of research. My scholarship examines how people live with chronic illness in public and private life. My professional work focuses on building the infrastructure, teams, and processes that let interdisciplinary research actually get done.

---

## Research

**Dissertation:** *Managing Visibility: Publics, Secrecy, and Moral Personhood in the Everyday Life of Type 1 Diabetes in Belize*

Drawing on medical anthropology, this project follows how people with Type 1 diabetes in Belize navigate disclosure, concealment, and the social meanings attached to a condition that is at once intimate and inescapably public.

## Research Program Management

**Everyday Respect** · University of Southern California
